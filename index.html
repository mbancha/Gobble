Quick rules

2 players: white center. 3–4: add light gray. 5–6: add dark gray arms. Start with one central special food.

Orange food stays printed. Gold super food: 2 growth. Multicolored special food: 2 growth + a card. Pickups disappear when eaten.

Score 1 per segment gained, or 2 per segment that cannot fit. Death keeps your score and drops food at head and tail only.

One personal boost each round. Growth spaces 3, 6 and 10 grant expendable extras.
